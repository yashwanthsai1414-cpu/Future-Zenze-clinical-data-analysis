# 🏥 Clinical Research Treatment Cost & Patient Outcome Analysis – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Clinical Research Data Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze the relationship between **treatment costs and patient outcomes**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of clinical treatment data by combining patient demographics, disease categories, treatment information, clinical sites, treatment costs, and patient outcome ratings.

The primary objective of this project is to identify **treatment effectiveness, cost variations, patient outcome trends, and potentially cost-effective treatment approaches** through interactive data visualization and analytical reporting.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **data cleaning, data transformation, data modelling, DAX, KPI development, data visualization, and healthcare analytics**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Clinical research organizations generate large volumes of patient and treatment data. Simply storing this information is not enough; researchers and decision-makers need to understand the relationship between **treatment expenditure and patient outcomes**.

The objective of this dashboard is to answer questions such as:

- Which treatment has the highest average cost?
- Which treatment provides better patient outcomes?
- What is the average treatment cost per patient?
- Which disease categories require higher treatment expenditure?
- How do patient outcome ratings vary across treatment groups?
- Is higher treatment cost associated with better patient outcomes?
- Which clinical sites have higher treatment costs?
- What percentage of patients achieved positive treatment outcomes?
- How do outcomes differ across age groups and patient demographics?
- Which treatment appears to provide a better balance between cost and outcome?

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Patient Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the patient population included in the clinical research dataset.

Analysis includes:

- Total number of patients
- Age distribution
- Gender distribution
- Disease category
- Treatment group
- Clinical site
- Patient outcome status

This section helps understand the overall composition of the study population.

---

### 2. Treatment Cost Analysis

The treatment cost analysis evaluates the financial aspect of different treatment approaches.

Key metrics include:

- Total treatment expenditure
- Average treatment cost
- Minimum and maximum treatment cost
- Treatment-wise cost comparison
- Disease-wise treatment cost
- Site-wise treatment cost
- Cost distribution across patient groups

This analysis helps identify treatments and disease categories associated with higher healthcare expenditure.

---

### 3. Patient Outcome Analysis

Patient outcomes are analyzed using outcome ratings recorded after treatment.

The dashboard evaluates:

- Average outcome rating
- Treatment-wise outcome rating
- Disease-wise outcome rating
- Outcome distribution
- Positive vs. negative outcomes
- Patient outcome trends
- Outcome performance across demographic groups

The analysis helps identify which treatment groups demonstrate stronger patient outcomes.
